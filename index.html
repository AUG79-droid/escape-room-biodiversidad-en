Mandatory mission briefing

The dashboard failed. Your reasoning is the recovery key.

A simulated project decision has fragmented the sustainability picture into ten connected systems. Recover them in order. Each room reveals one letter and unlocks a technical debrief. Combine the letters to enter the final recovery code.

How to play

  1. Enter Room 1. Later rooms remain locked.
  2. Complete every field and validate your response.
  3. Each room allows five complete attempts; incomplete answers do not count.
  4. After a fifth incorrect attempt, the correct solution is revealed and the learning route continues.
  5. Read each debrief, collect the ten clues and enter the final code.

Your progress is stored in this browser. There is no time penalty: the timer records duration only.
