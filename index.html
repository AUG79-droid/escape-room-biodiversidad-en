Mandatory mission briefing

The dashboard failed. Your reasoning is the recovery key.

A simulated project decision has fragmented the sustainability picture into five separate systems. Recover them in order. Each solved room reveals one letter and unlocks a technical debrief. Combine the letters to enter the final recovery code.

How to play

  1. Enter Room 1. Later rooms remain locked.
  2. Complete every field and validate your response.
  3. If the answer is wrong, use the targeted feedback or reveal the optional hint.
  4. After solving, read the debrief before entering the next room.
  5. Combine the five clues in room order and enter the final code.

Your progress is stored in this browser. There is no time penalty: the timer records duration only.
